## 프롬프트 (1번)

```
You are a top Korean nail artist presenting a design set.
Create ONE photorealistic top-down flat-lay photo of a press-on nail tip sample board: individual medium almond nail tips laid out in neat rows on a plain light-grey background, soft even studio lighting.

DESIGN BRIEF — follow every line exactly:
- Base of every tip: sheer translucent colour built up in several layers, so looking at the tip feels like looking into a cross-section of polished mineral.
- Structure: translucent colour is built up in layers across the whole tip, so depth comes from the stack rather than from any boundary line
- Negative space: 20-40% of each tip stays free of motifs.
- Length rule (medium tips): the layered colour spreads across the whole tip with no boundary line; three or four layers of sheer colour build the depth.
- TEXTURE: Sheer jelly and milky colours are layered over a magnetic cat-eye base, so one soft light streak moves underneath the translucent layers.
- TEXTURE: Soft swirls are dragged through the wet sheer layers, their edges blurring into one another with no hard line anywhere.
- TEXTURE: Fine chrome lines trace a few of the swirl boundaries, catching light like a mineral vein.
- Finish: One high-gloss finish over the whole set; the depth comes from the layers underneath rather than from the top coat.
- Every tip is a different draw from the same mineral: the same colours layered in a different order, so no two tips repeat.
- Colour boundaries are always soft — where two colours meet they bleed into each other over a millimetre or more.
- One or two tips carry a fine chrome vein tracing the edge of a swirl, and nothing else.
- Earth-and-water colours dominate: forest green, warm brown, deep-sea blue, smoky amethyst.
- Palette: milky white (#f4f3f0, 35% of the surface — the ground colour every tip starts from); baby pink (#ecaec0, 25% of the surface — a colour the design is drawn in); gothic black (#111111, 20% of the surface — a colour the design is drawn in); silver chrome (#c2c4c6, 20% of the surface — a small-quantity accent for lines, dots, and metal).
- Motifs to use, each keeping exactly the material named here: metal piercing rings rendered as metal at standard scale; gothic cross charms rendered as metal at standard scale.
- Keep every shape and placement identical and rotate which colour goes where, so each tip wears a different member of the same palette.
- Tip shape almond, length medium. This is the client's order and it holds for every tip on the board.
- PART PHYSICS: any part is a tiny flat metal bead lying flush ON the surface, sealed under clear gel so it reads as one of the layers rather than an object added on top.
- Parts budget for the whole set: 0 statement parts plus 0-2 small studs or beads in total.
- This set stays clear of: hard-edged geometric motifs, opaque flat colour blocking, a french boundary line, crisp outlines around shapes, glitter particles.
- Every element is physically buildable by hand with gel, powder, film, and attachable parts, each part sealed under a layer of clear gel.
- The render reads as a photograph of real finished nail tips: each tip's own outline is crisp and unwarped, and every material renders honestly, with no melted or smeared silhouette edges.
- Keep believable hand-made character: micro-variations between tips, natural gel thickness and edge highlights, not computer-perfect symmetry.
- Keep it abstract nail art: no eyes, eyeballs, iris or pupil shapes, no faces.
- Mood: An ultra-maximalist fusion of coquette sweetness and punk-rock edge.
- The tips are the only subject: plain background, clean composition, no text overlays, nothing else in frame.
```
